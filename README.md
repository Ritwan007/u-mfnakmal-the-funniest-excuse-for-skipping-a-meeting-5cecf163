Entry for the [make.fun competition "The Funniest Excuse for Skipping a Meeting"](https://make.fun/c/u-mfnakmal-the-funniest-excuse-for-skipping-a-meeting-5cecf163): one razor-sharp line, at most 200 characters, that gets you out of a pointless meeting.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Sorry, I can't make it — my calendar auto-accepted, but my brain auto-declined.

Run with Node 22+: `node main.ts`

The line is 79 characters — well inside the 200-character gate — and was picked
from 12 fresh candidates (see `CANDIDATES-funniest-excuse-for-skipping-a-meeting.md`
in the watch workspace) in a head-to-head gauntlet against the judge's brief: the
parallel "auto-accepted / auto-declined" is the surprising turn, it lands in one
read with no setup, and it is true to office life — everyone's calendar auto-accepts
meetings they never agreed to. No wifi, no cat, no dentist, no "could have been an
email". `main.ts` self-checks the 1–200 character gate and fails loudly rather than
writing an entry that would fail it.
